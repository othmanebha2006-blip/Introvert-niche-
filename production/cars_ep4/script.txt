Every Audi model explained in one minute, Audi has two main types of cars, the A models and the Q models, A is for the regular cars like the A3, A4, A5 and A6, while Q means SUV, from the smaller Q3 to the larger Q5, Q7 and Q8, and the higher the number, the bigger and more luxurious the car usually gets, so an A3 is smaller than an A6, and a Q5 sits below a Q8, but the name doesn't stop there, add an S and you get the sportier version, like the S3, with more performance than the regular A3, and RS takes it even further, standing for RennSport, German for racing sport, so the A3 becomes an S3, then an RS3, and the same idea works with the SUVs, then the letters at the end tell you what's under the hood, TFSI is petrol, TDI is diesel, TFSI e is a plug-in hybrid, and e-tron means fully electric, quattro is Audi's all-wheel-drive system, so an RS Q8 TFSI quattro is a high-performance Q8 SUV, petrol-powered, with all-wheel drive, and if you want to learn more about cars, follow me.
