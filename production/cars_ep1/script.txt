Every Lexus model explained in one minute, Lexus names usually start with two letters, and those letters tell you what kind of car it is, IS and ES are saloons, the IS is the smaller sportier one, while the ES is the more comfortable executive car, LS is the flagship luxury saloon, and put an X at the end and you get an SUV, UX is the urban crossover, NX is the nimble crossover, RX is the larger radiant crossover, GX is the rugged off-roader, and LX is the flagship SUV, then comes the number, like 350 or 500, which used to roughly relate to engine size, but today it can also reflect the powertrain and performance, an h means hybrid, h plus means plug-in hybrid, and e means fully electric, so an RX 500h is a hybrid SUV, not a 5-litre engine, then there's F Sport, which adds a sportier look and feel, but F is different, that's the high-performance division, like the RC F, and the F comes from Fuji Speedway in Japan, Lexus also uses the letter L for its luxury-focused models, like the LS and LX, while the LC is its grand touring coupe, designed for long journeys with a sporty feel. And the LFA was Lexus's legendary V10 supercar, showing that the brand could build something far beyond luxury saloons and SUVs.
