Every Mercedes model explained in one minute, Mercedes uses letters to show you where each car sits in the lineup, the A Class is the entry point, while the B Class is taller and more practical, move up to the C Class and you get a compact luxury saloon, the E Class is larger and more executive, and the S Class is the flagship, the most luxurious saloon Mercedes makes, but the same letters can become SUVs, put GL in front and you get the GLA, GLB, GLC, GLE and GLS, following a similar size order, the GLA is the smallest, while the GLS sits at the top of the SUV range, with space and luxury closer to the S Class, the G Class is different, that's the boxy off-roader known as the G Wagon, then there's CL, used for coupe-style models, like the CLA, the smaller four-door coupe, and the CLE, which replaces the C and E Class coupes and convertibles, the SL is Mercedes' luxury roadster, while the AMG GT is the dedicated performance sports car, so the letters tell you whether you're looking at an entry-level car, a flagship saloon, an SUV, a coupe or a sports car, and if you want to learn more about cars, follow me.
