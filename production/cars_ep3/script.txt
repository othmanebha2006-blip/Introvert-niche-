Audi model names explained in one minute, Audi uses two main letters to separate its cars, A is for the lower-bodied models like the A3 and A6, while Q is for the SUVs, from the compact Q3 to the larger Q7 and Q8, the number tells you where the car sits in the range, higher usually means bigger and more expensive, but the number doesn't tell you the engine, that's where the badges come in, TFSI is petrol, TDI is diesel, and e-tron means fully electric, while e-hybrid is a plug-in hybrid, the body style comes next, Avant is the estate, Sportback gives you a sloping rear roof, and Sedan is the saloon, then there's the performance side, S means a sporty model, and RS comes from RennSport, German for racing sport, so an A5 can become an S5, then an RS 5, but S line is different, it's a styling package with sportier details, not a full S model, quattro is Audi's all-wheel-drive system, and newer models are moving away from the old power numbers like 40 and 45, because the name is now focused on the car's size and position, so an RS 6 Avant performance quattro is a high-performance Audi estate, with the sharper RS setup, extra performance, and all-wheel drive, and if you want to learn more about cars, follow me.
